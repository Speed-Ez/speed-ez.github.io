About the work

I work with data where it meets real operations and decisions. Most of my time goes into structuring and maintaining data systems, cleaning messy operational data, building reporting layers, and making sure the numbers people rely on are actually usable.

Core tools: SQL, Python, Power BI, Excel, PostgreSQL/Supabase, Power Query, and the usual data stack. I care more about reliable systems and clear operational insight than about producing charts for the sake of charts.

Selected Projects

Car Insurance Analysis — Onyx DataDNA

Analyzed ~37,500 policyholders and 10,300 claimants (27.5% claim rate, ~$518M in payouts). Focused on claim behavior, risk patterns, and practical insights for better risk assessment and portfolio decisions. Built with SQL and Power BI.

University Data Management System

Part of the team that designed and implemented a university-wide data management system in roughly four months after years without one. Work covered data modeling, cleaning, operational reporting, user support, and ongoing maintenance of the live system (PostgreSQL / Supabase).

Media Buying Economics Monitor

Internal profitability and performance system for a traffic-arbitrage operation. Tracks daily spend, revenue, eCPM, ROI, and page-level results. Includes a cost calculator and automated daily alerts so issues surface without someone having to open a dashboard first.

TPI Energy Consumption Report

Turned raw energy consumption logs into usable planning insights using data preprocessing, DAX measures, and Power BI. Focused on making the data actionable for operational decisions rather than just descriptive reporting.

Nashville Housing — Data Cleaning (SQL)

Full cleaning and standardization of a messy real-estate dataset in SQL. Handled duplicates, inconsistent formats, missing values, and structural issues so the data could actually be used for analysis.

COVID-19 Data Exploration (SQL + Power BI)

Explored global case and death data with SQL, then layered Power BI visualizations on top. Focused on clear comparisons across continents and countries and the practical questions the numbers could answer.

Call Agent Performance (AHT)

Analyzed call-center agent performance with a focus on Average Handle Time and related metrics. Produced a clear evaluation of effectiveness that could actually be used for coaching and operational adjustments.

Used Car Price Analysis

Explored how vehicle features drive selling price and built a predictive model. Emphasis was on understanding the market drivers and producing something useful for pricing decisions, not just model accuracy.

Hotel Booking Analysis (2010–2019)

Summarized ten years of booking history across countries and hotels. Focused on patterns that could support capacity and marketing decisions rather than pure historical description.

Sprocket Central — Customer Targeting

Built a dashboard and analysis to help prioritise which new customers to target from a 1,000-person list. Combined sales history with clear recommendations for the business.

Pharma Wholesale & Retail Performance

Four-year sales analysis covering two countries, team performance, and product-level results. Aimed at giving management a clearer view of what was actually driving (or dragging) results.

Breast Cancer Classification

Machine-learning classification project reaching ~98% accuracy. Included as an example of applied modeling work; the process emphasized understanding the data and trade-offs rather than chasing the highest possible score.
